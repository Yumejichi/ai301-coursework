I made my first contribution to AIDJ, an open-source AI DJ app, by adding missing navigation links on desktop and mobile recently so I want to share it! I also used an AI tool to check my work before submitting a pull request.

The change makes Tasks easier to find on desktop and mobile, and Library Growth easier to find on mobile, by adding the missing links to the navigation menus. I used Codex to help prepare tests, reviewed them, and wrote and checked the navigation changes myself. When I submitted the PR through the checker, it required a disclosure heading the project didn’t ask for. I revised the rule and clarified how AI helped, and the next check passed.

The maintainer reviewed the change and approved and merged my pull request! They also said the before-and-after screenshots and separate test commit made review easier.

This taught me that contributing involves more than writing code: clear evidence, focused changes, and checking AI suggestions matter too. Also using AI to help check the changes and documentation is helpful and efficient.

I also want to thank the maintainer for guiding my first contribution to AIDJ!
